# `balance`

`balance` changes how a team's rating combines its players. Default:
`false`. It applies to every model.

## What it does

Without `balance`, a team's `mu` is the sum of its players' `mu`, and its
variance is the sum of their variances. With `balance: true`, each player
counts more the further their [ordinal](/tuning/ordinal) is below the best
ordinal on the team. The factor is `1 + (best − own) / best`. The best
player counts once.

The result: an uneven team looks stronger, and less certain, than the sum of
its parts. So it gains less from a win and loses more from a loss. The
players on the team still move by the same amount, because each update
scales by the player's own `sigma²` over the team's.

The factor divides by the best ordinal. When that ordinal is near zero, the
factor is huge, and when it is negative, the factor can be negative too.
New players with the default rating have an ordinal of exactly 0. So use
`balance` only when the best player on every team has a clearly positive
ordinal.

## When to change it

- Turn `balance` on when strong players win too much rating with weak
  teammates.
- Keep it off for new players, or when ordinals can be near zero.

```js
rate(teams, { balance: true })
```
