# `margin`

`margin` makes the size of a win matter. Default: none. It needs `score`,
and it applies to every model.

## What it does

`rate` first rates the game by the order of the scores. Then, for each team,
it computes a factor from the score differences to the other teams. Each
difference above `margin` adds `log1p(difference − margin)`, and each
difference inside the margin adds 0. The factor is 1 plus the average of
these. `rate` multiplies each player's change in `mu` by the factor. `sigma`
is not affected.

- A difference inside the margin gives a factor of 1: a plain win.
- The factor grows with the logarithm of the excess, so a win by 40 points
  is not twice a win by 20.
- With two teams and a difference of `margin + 10`, the factor is
  `1 + ln(11)`, about 3.4.

## When to change it

- Set `margin` to the score difference that separates a close game from a
  clear one, in the points of your game.
- `margin` must be more than 0. With 0, or without `score`, nothing is
  amplified.

```js
rate(teams, { score: [37, 12], margin: 10 })
```
