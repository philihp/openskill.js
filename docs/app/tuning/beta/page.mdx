# `beta`

`beta` is the spread of a player's performance from game to game. Default:
`25/6`, about 4.17. It applies to every model.

## What it does

A player with mean `mu` does not perform at `mu` in every game. `beta` is the
standard deviation of that performance. The models add `beta²` per team to
the uncertainty of a result, next to the `sigma²` of each player.

- A small `beta` makes results predictable. A rating gap then gives a large
  win chance. An expected win moves the ratings a little, and an upset moves
  them a lot.
- A large `beta` makes results noisy. Even a large gap gives a modest win
  chance, and each result moves the ratings by a smaller, more equal amount.

Move the `beta` slider and watch the slope of both curves. Move the gap to

## When to change it

- Lower `beta` when the better player wins almost every time, as in a
  sprint.
- Raise `beta` when luck plays a large part, as in a card game.
- Keep `beta` in proportion to `sigma`. If you change the scale of `mu` and
  `sigma`, scale `beta` by the same factor.

```js
rate(teams, { beta: 2 })
```
