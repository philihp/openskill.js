# `gamma`

`gamma` sets how fast `sigma` shrinks after a game. Default: `sigma / c`,
where `c` is the total uncertainty of the game. It applies to every model.

## What it does

A result changes a team's `mu` by `omega` and shrinks its `sigma` by
`delta`. `gamma` multiplies `delta` only. So `gamma` does not change the
first update to `mu`. But a larger `gamma` lowers `sigma` faster, and later
results then move `mu` less.

The default, `sigma / c`, shrinks `sigma` less when the rest of the game is
uncertain.

You pass a function: `(c, k, mu, sigmaSq, team, rank) => number`. `c` is the
total uncertainty of the game, `k` is the number of teams, `mu` and `sigmaSq`
are the team's rating, `team` is its players and `rank` is its rank.

## When to change it

- Use a multiplier below 1 to keep players uncertain for longer, and above 1
  to settle them faster.
- Try [`tau`](/tuning/tau) first. It sets a floor for `sigma`, which is often
  what you want.
- A very large `gamma` reaches the [`kappa`](/tuning/epsilon) floor.

```js
rate(teams, { gamma: (c, k, mu, sigmaSq) => (2 * Math.sqrt(sigmaSq)) / c })
```
